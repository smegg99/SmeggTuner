## Who is responsible

SmeggTuner is developed by smegg99. This policy explains how the SmeggTuner Android app (package `me.smegg.smeggtuner`), its desktop and browser editions, and smeggtuner.com handle information. Features differ between editions.

For privacy questions or requests, contact smegg99 at [support@smegg.me](mailto:support@smegg.me). You do not need an app account to contact the developer.

## Microphone and audio files

SmeggTuner uses microphone audio to calculate pitch, reed frequencies and beats on your device. On Android, it requests microphone permission. You can refuse or revoke that permission in Android's app settings; live microphone tuning will then be unavailable. Opening an audio file does not require microphone access.

Audio analysis runs locally. SmeggTuner does not upload microphone audio, imported audio files or tuning measurements to the developer, an analytics service or an advertising service. Live audio is held in memory for analysis; starting the tuner does not automatically save a microphone recording. On Android, capture and generated tones stop when the app leaves the foreground.

When you choose a WAV file, SmeggTuner reads that file for playback and analysis. Android's document picker controls which file the app can access. A selected cloud storage provider may download the file according to that provider's own practices. Temporary local copies may remain in the app's cache until Android or you clear it.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps preferences such as language, theme, reference pitch and audio calibration locally. Its local database can also contain saved tuning work from editions that provide those features.

Technical logs can contain app errors, device or audio-device details, settings and file paths. Logs are kept locally, including Android's system log. The app does not automatically upload these logs or send crash reports to the developer. If you choose to send a log or screenshot for support, review it first and remove anything you do not want to share.

The Android app uses private app storage. It does not provide an additional encryption layer for its local database or files; protection also depends on your device's security settings. Android backup and device transfer are enabled. Depending on your Android version, device and backup settings, the operating system may copy app data to your backup provider or another device. The developer does not receive those backups. You can manage them through your device and backup provider.

## Network connections, accounts and third parties

The Android tuner works without an account or an internet connection once installed. It has no advertising SDK, in-app analytics tracker, account registration or automatic upload of tuning data. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos library or advertising identifier. Choosing a file grants access to that selected document, rather than your entire storage.

The Android edition does not perform the desktop edition's GitHub update checks. Google Play manages installation and updates of the Play edition under Google's policies. Google or the device manufacturer may independently process store, operating-system, backup or diagnostic information according to your settings and their policies.

On desktop, the optional update checker contacts GitHub over HTTPS with the app name and version to look for releases. GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address. You can turn off these checks in Settings. Audio and tuning files are not part of that request.

Optional links to the online policy, Website and GitHub open an external browser. The policy itself can be read inside the app without a connection. Support email links open your email application; a message is sent only if you choose to send it. The destination receives ordinary web request information, including your IP address and browser details, and applies its own policy. Donations are handled by the linked provider; SmeggTuner does not receive your payment card details. If an edition lets you export or print a document, the destination or print service you choose receives that document.

## The website and browser edition

Visiting smeggtuner.com or loading the browser edition involves requests to the hosting infrastructure. Connection and security information can include IP address, requested URL, time, browser information and errors. Hosting and security providers process this information to deliver the service and prevent abuse. Cloudflare provides web traffic protection; its processing is described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The website uses cookie-free Umami statistics when analytics is enabled. These can include visited pages, referring sites, browser and device categories, approximate location, clicks on tagged links and page performance. Umami processes connection information to distinguish visits; cookie-free does not mean that no information is processed. The analytics endpoint is served through this website. These website statistics are separate from the Android app and contain no microphone audio or tuning measurements.

The site stores your theme preference in browser storage and can use a language-preference cookie. The contact page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to limit automated requests; it processes browser and connection signals as explained in the [Turnstile privacy notice](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/). Reading this privacy policy does not require completing the contact challenge.

The browser tuner processes microphone audio locally in your browser after browser permission. It does not upload that audio for analysis. Browser permissions and site-storage controls let you revoke access and clear local preferences.

## Support, retention and deletion

If you contact the developer, the information you choose to send, your email address and your correspondence are used to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Do not send recordings, personal information or sensitive documents unless they are needed for your request. Information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Local settings and saved work remain until you remove them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. On Android, use Settings > Apps > SmeggTuner > Storage > Clear storage to reset app data; names vary by device. Clearing the cache removes temporary files but does not reset all settings. Uninstalling or clearing the app does not delete files you exported elsewhere, copies you sent to another person, or backups held by another service. Manage those copies separately.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to answer the request, follow up on the issue or meet applicable legal obligations. Website operational and security records are retained for service operation, abuse prevention and investigation; visit statistics are retained for understanding and improving the website. Retention depends on those purposes and the relevant service settings. You can ask about or request deletion of information held by the developer through the privacy contact above. The developer cannot remotely access or delete files held only on your device.
